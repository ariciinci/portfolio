# About how things come together.
I'm a multidisciplinary creative with a background spanning digital design, art direction, ecommerce, campaign production and creative operations.

Over the years, I've worked on global and multi-market work for brands including adidas, Reebok, Calvin Klein, Amazon, Philips and Virgin Media. My role has often been translating creative and marketing direction into work that designers, producers, developers and regional teams can actually deliver.

I started as a hands-on designer, so I understand the work from the inside. I've designed digital products and experiences, developed campaign concepts, directed creative work and supported large-scale ecommerce launches. As my responsibilities grew, I became increasingly involved in the wider system around the work: production, localisation, stakeholder alignment, workflows, quality assurance and delivery across markets. 

I can move between the creative detail and the bigger operational picture — understanding what the work needs to achieve, where complexity is getting in the way, and what a team needs in order to move forward.

My background in film, animation and graphic design has also shaped how I think. I'm naturally visual, based on analytical thinking. Connecting the narrative, systems and how different pieces connect within different platforms, formats and profiles . Working internationally has made me comfortable collaborating across disciplines, cultures and different ways of working.

Today, I'm particularly interested in roles across creative production, creative operations, campaign and content delivery, and digital transformation. I'm also exploring how AI and automation can remove repetitive work and create more space for the parts of the creative process that benefit from human judgement.

After many years in the field, I've become comfortable with complexity and change. I don't expect every project to begin with perfect clarity. I'm good at finding the structure within it, bringing the right people together and helping turn an idea into something that can actually be delivered.
