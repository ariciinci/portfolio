# About how things come together.
I'm a creative operations manager with a background in digital design, art direction, ecommerce and campaign production.

I've worked on global and multi-market projects for brands including adidas, Reebok, Calvin Klein, Amazon, Philips and Virgin Media. My experience spans creative direction, production and delivery, translating business and marketing objectives into clear processes and deliverables.

Having started as a hands-on designer, I understand the creative process from concept to execution. Today, I focus on connecting people, processes and creative direction to make complex projects work across teams, channels and markets.

With a background in film, animation and graphic design, I combine visual thinking with an analytical approach. I'm comfortable working across disciplines, managing complexity and bringing structure to changing environments.

I'm particularly interested in creative operations, production, campaign delivery and digital transformation, including how AI and automation can make creative workflows more efficient.
