## FROM DIGITAL DESIGN TO CREATIVE PRODUCTION

<a id="section-1-1"></a>

### Role Evolution

I joined the Reebok ecommerce team at adidas HQ in Amsterdam to cover the Creative Manager's maternity leave. While my official title was Senior Interactive Designer, my role expanded into Creative Production and Campaign Delivery, working closely with Production and Marketing.

Alongside creative development, I became involved in campaign planning, asset production, creative resources, external partners, stakeholder alignment and workflow development.

The role increasingly became about connecting creative work with the wider operation required to deliver global campaigns.

## GLOBAL CAMPAIGN DELIVERY

The Reebok Holiday Campaign was a phased global ecommerce activation across EMEA, NA, LATAM and APAC, spanning multiple digital channels, up to 11 languages and hundreds of localised assets.

Working with the internal Creative, Production and Marketing teams, external studios, freelancers and localisation partners, I helped move the campaign from approved creative direction into global production and delivery.

### From Concept to Production

Following creative sign-off, I developed the first campaign blueprint and coordinated the transition into production.

The blueprint became a shared reference for creative direction, messaging, asset requirements and localisation, giving the teams involved a clearer foundation for production and review.

## BUILDING A MORE STRUCTURED CREATIVE OPERATION

As campaign complexity increased, I became more involved in how Creative worked with Marketing, Production, UX/CX, regional teams and external partners.

I introduced and developed practical structures around recurring areas of the campaign process: campaign blueprints, reviews, stakeholder guidance, handovers, onboarding, resource planning and creative forecasting.

Rather than supporting a single activation, these structures evolved across campaigns and helped make information clearer, responsibilities more visible and delivery more repeatable.

<a id="section-3-1"></a>

### Connecting Global Teams

Global delivery required continuous coordination between Creative, Marketing, Production, UX/CX, Analytics, Localisation, regional markets, agencies and freelancers.

I worked across these teams throughout the campaign lifecycle, supporting communication, reviews, approvals, production and delivery.

For localisation, this meant coordinating the movement from approved campaign direction through translation, production, creative QA and final market delivery while protecting brand consistency.

## FROM CAMPAIGN DELIVERY TO A MORE RELIABLE CREATIVE FUNCTION

Across repeated activations, the work contributed to a more structured and predictable way of operating: clearer campaign documentation, more consistent reviews, smoother localisation handovers, better visibility of creative resources and less reliance on informal knowledge.

By combining creative production with workflow development, reporting and cross-functional coordination, Creative became better integrated with the wider campaign operation.

For me, the role marked a shift from primarily creating digital work to understanding and improving the systems, teams and processes required to deliver it at global scale.
