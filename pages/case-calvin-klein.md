# Digital Campaign Production
**Calvin Klein · Freelance · Amsterdam · June 2022**

I joined Calvin Klein in June 2022 on a short-term freelance assignment, covering for a senior art director who was away. The team already had established creative and production processes in place — campaigns were being produced through existing workflows, templates, automation and delivery structures, so the first priority was to understand how the system worked and become productive quickly.

My role was focused on digital asset production and campaign delivery support. I worked from approved photography, creative direction and existing campaign assets, adapting them into the digital formats required by the briefs. During the assignment I supported several campaign activations, including EOSS, CK Jeans Spring, June Refresh, CK Pride Photo Booth and Geofencing Local Pride Moments. The largest production assignment was EOSS, where I personally produced and exported 300+ digital assets in approximately two weeks.

## The Assignment — Stepping into a System That Was Already Running
This wasn't a project where I was asked to create a new visual direction or develop a new production process. The creative direction and campaign material were already established; my responsibility was to take that approved material and turn it into the digital outputs required by the campaign. Where the brief didn't contain all the technical information needed, I'd check the relevant project information, existing assets or platform requirements and work out what was required before producing the asset.

**Worked closely with:** Production Manager, Creative Producer, Creative/Art Direction team, Marketing stakeholders

## The Production Environment
Calvin Klein already had a structured digital production environment, with predefined templates, established campaign structures and automation methods that helped the team produce large quantities of digital assets consistently.

**Tools/methods:** Adobe XD, Photoshop Actions, automated resizing and exporting, predefined templates, existing campaign and live assets

## From Brief to Asset
The production process started with the brief and the assets available for the campaign. I worked from approved photography and creative direction, using existing templates or production methods, and investigated any missing technical detail separately. I worked within Calvin Klein's Wrike project-management system, and referred back to relevant Slack conversations and the original brief to make sure I was working from the latest information.

## EOSS — 300+ Assets in Approximately Two Weeks
EOSS — End of Season Sale — was the clearest example of production volume during the assignment. During my production period, I personally produced and exported 300+ digital assets in approximately two weeks, covering Web, E-commerce, Email, Paid Social and Instagram Stories.

The campaign moved through different stages, and the layouts I produced included work for stages such as: Main Sale → Further Reductions → Final Reductions → Last Chance → Final Hours. The production challenge was a combination of volume, variation and timing — the underlying campaign creative needed to remain consistent, while individual assets had to be adapted to their particular placement and the current stage of the promotion.

## A Campaign Across Different Digital Formats
The work included examples across desktop landing pages, mobile layouts, e-commerce, email/newsletter, Instagram feed, Instagram Stories, paid social and collection advertising. The creative language remained connected across the campaign, but individual assets needed to be adapted to the requirements of each environment. The scale of the work came from working systematically with approved campaign material, existing templates and production methods — not from designing hundreds of unrelated pieces from scratch.

## Quality Assurance
At this volume, checking the work was part of the production itself. Before sending assets for review, I'd check that current requirements had been followed, review relevant feedback, and confirm applicable platform requirements, correct file naming, and necessary sign-off were in place before delivery. I don't have a reliable error-rate or rework percentage from the assignment, so I wouldn't attach a number to the quality of the work — what I can say is that I developed the habit of checking the source information again before considering an asset ready to move forward.

## A Small Production Issue
During production, I noticed that some existing social-media templates were following outdated platform requirements. I raised the issue with the internal production/creative team and discussed it with the Production Manager, including the deadline impact of making the change. Once the approach was agreed, I updated the existing templates, retained the previous versions, and communicated the change during a production meeting so the wider team understood the difference once assets moved into activation.

## Working with the Team
Although my role was focused on asset production, I worked closely with the Production Manager and Creative Producer, and interacted with the Creative/Art Direction team and Marketing stakeholders as the work moved through review. The team already knew my previous digital-design experience, including work for brands such as adidas and Reebok, which helped establish trust in my ability to step into the environment and contribute quickly.

## From Production to Handoff
Once assets were reviewed and signed off, I supplied master assets to Calvin Klein's localisation agencies. My responsibility was to make sure the assets I supplied were correct, followed the brief and had the required sign-off — localisation itself was handled downstream. I wasn't responsible for the overall campaign strategy, creative direction or localisation; my responsibility was to produce the digital assets accurately, keep them moving through the established process and make sure the work I handed over was ready for the next stage.

*Production, judgement, attention to detail and reliable handoff is what I take from the experience.*
